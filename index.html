How to Install (macOS Security)

Because this is a brand-new indie app, macOS requires a quick manual approval on your first launch.

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag the app to your Applications folder.
  2. Try to open the app. If you see an "unidentified developer" warning, click OK.
  3. Open your Mac's System Settings > Privacy & Security.
  4. Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to the app name.

Features

Everything you wished
macOS did natively.

🗂

Instant Organisation

Take a screenshot, and SortShot routes it directly into clean, designated folders. Your desktop stays spotless without you lifting a finger.

🔗

It Remembers the Source

SortShot grabs the active URL when you take a screenshot and stores it in the file metadata. One click gets you right back to the original page.

🔍

Find Anything, Instantly

Looking for that one specific UI reference in an ocean of files? Use the built-in visual timeline to find exactly what you need.

✂️

Zero-Clutter Sharing

Annotate your screenshot, copy it to your clipboard, and SortShot instantly trashes the file. Quick shares that never clutter your hard drive.

📁

Custom Projects

Working on a redesign or a research paper? Group your visual inspiration, references, and assets into dedicated, color-coded project spaces.

⌨️

Menu Bar Native

SortShot lives quietly in your menu bar and intercepts your existing screenshot hotkeys — ⌘⇧3 and ⌘⇧4 — with zero friction.
